Opening setup

A borrowed lite is a 4-sided frame: head, jambs and a 2", 4" or 9 1/2" base, set at the sill height above the floor. Add rails (2" or 8" each) and vertical mullions under Mullions & rails with +, type a CL to move one, or draw them on the sheet. Lite sizes, glass sizes and the glass schedule update automatically.

Handing is viewed from the outside (keyed side): LH/RH swing away from you, LHR/RHR swing toward you. Sizes are daylight openings. Lite heights are the glass sizes top to bottom; each comma adds a horizontal mullion. Lite widths work left to right and add vertical mullions. auto fills what's left (e.g. 24, auto). In each sidelite, Mid rail CL and Vertical mullion lists place rails and verticals by location (+ adds more), and Base height above floor raises the sidelite off the floor.
